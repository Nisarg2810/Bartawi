This is a working mock up, not a live system. Nothing here is saved beyond this browser tab. It's meant to show what a digital material recovery log could look like for Bartawi's C&D waste operation: a manifest per load, rolling into a site level diversion view, in place of paper tickets or an end of month spreadsheet reconstruction.
